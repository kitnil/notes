** Damage
- Added damage (usually only for attacks only)
- Skill conversion (e.g. skill converts physical to cold damage)
- Conversion and gain damage as (two phases at the same time)
** Mobs resists
=Red= means monster is vulnerable to the damage type.

=Grey= (=Silver=) means monster has a resist to the damage type.

=Yellow= (=Gold=) means monster has a big resist to the damage type.
